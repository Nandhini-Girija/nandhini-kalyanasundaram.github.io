Research

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, signal processing, and scientific computing. I am interested in developing principled learning-based methods for solving inverse problems and uncertainty-aware reconstruction tasks.

Research Areas

  • Diffusion Models
  • Inverse Problems
  • Scientific Machine Learning
  • Bayesian Inference
  • Generative AI
  • Computational Imaging
  • Signal Processing

Current Research

Currently, I am working on diffusion posterior sampling, score-based generative models, and uncertainty quantification for imaging inverse problems.

My research aims to develop trustworthy AI systems for scientific imaging, sensing, and reconstruction applications.
