About Me

I am currently a PhD Scholar at IIT Madras working on diffusion models, inverse problems, probabilistic generative modeling, and scientific machine learning.

My research focuses on developing principled generative methods for solving inverse problems in imaging and sensing systems.

Prior to joining IIT Madras, I completed my M.Tech in Signal Processing at NIT Calicut with a CGPA of 9.88 and received the Gold Medal.

Research Interests

  • Diffusion Models
  • Inverse Problems
  • Scientific Machine Learning
  • Bayesian Inference
  • Signal Processing
