About Me

I am a PhD Scholar in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, where my research is intersection of machine learning, inverse problems, and computational imaging. My work focuses on developing principled generative modeling approaches for solving challenging scientific and engineering problems. In particular, I am interested in diffusion models, Bayesian inference, uncertainty quantification, and learning-based reconstruction methods for imaging and sensing applications. I am fascinated by the question of how modern generative models can be leveraged not only to produce realistic data, but also to perform reliable scientific inference in settings where measurements are incomplete, noisy, or indirect. My research aims to bridge the gap between statistical learning theory and practical reconstruction algorithms that can be deployed in real-world scientific systems. Prior to joining IIT Madras, I completed my M.Tech in Signal Processing at the National Institute of Technology Calicut, graduating with receiving the Institute Gold Medal for academic excellence. My long-term goal is to contribute to the development of trustworthy AI systems for scientific discovery, computational imaging, autonomous sensing, and next-generation intelligent technologies.

Research Interests

  • Inverse Problems
  • Scientific Machine Learning
  • Bayesian Inference
