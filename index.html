About Me

I am currently a PhD Scholar at IIT Madras working on diffusion models, inverse problems, probabilistic generative modeling, and scientific machine learning.

My research focuses on developing principled generative methods for solving inverse problems in imaging and sensing systems. I am particularly interested in diffusion-based generative models, Bayesian inference, uncertainty quantification, and scientific AI.

Prior to joining IIT Madras, I completed my M.Tech in Signal Processing at NIT Calicut, graduating with a Gold Medal and a CGPA of 9.88.

My long-term goal is to build trustworthy AI systems for scientific discovery, imaging, and autonomous sensing applications.

Research Interests

  • Diffusion Models
  • Inverse Problems
  • Scientific Machine Learning
  • Bayesian Inference
  • Signal Processing
  • Probabilistic Generative Modeling

Current Research

My current work investigates diffusion posterior sampling, uncertainty-aware reconstruction methods, and generative modeling approaches for scientific imaging and inverse problems.
